## 7. Key Insights

### 7.1 Network Topology

- **OctopusTakopi** has the richest network (50 following), spanning HFT practitioners, quant academics, exchange operators (Tether/Bitfinex CTO), and ML researchers. This is the most productive network node.
- **beinghorizontal** has a minimal network (follows only 2) but high follower count (375), indicating they are a destination rather than a connector. Their connection to **marketcalls** (openalgo, 1.5k stars) is notable.
- **focus1691** follows 35 users but the following list is inaccessible (profile 404 on web scrape). Their repos are accessible and highly relevant.

### 7.2 Technology Stack Patterns

| Stack | Who Uses It | Repos |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Rust + egui/eframe** | DegenSugarBoo | OpenBook (GPU-accelerated heatmaps) |
| **Rust + iced** | flowsurface-rs | flowsurface (native desktop charting) |
| **Rust + WASM** | cyanly | lbplot-rs (browser-based heatmap) |
| **TypeScript/NestJS** | focus1691 | orderflow, indicator-library |
| **Python + Dash/Plotly** | beinghorizontal | tpo_project, tpo_btc |
| **JavaScript + D3** | Elenchev | order-book-heatmap |

### 7.3 Most Valuable Discoveries

1. **flowsurface-rs/flowsurface** (1,400 stars, Rust) - The most feature-complete open-source trading platform found. Covers heatmaps, footprints, DOM/ladder, time & sales, candlesticks. Supports Binance, Bybit, Hyperliquid, OKX. Not directly in any of the 3 developers' networks but surfaces through their ecosystem.

2. **DegenSugarBoo/OpenBook** (122 stars, Rust) - Followed by OctopusTakopi. GPU-accelerated depth heatmap with trade tape. Most relevant for HD Heatmaps feature.

3. **nkaz001/hftbacktest** (3,800 stars, Python/Rust) - Forked by OctopusTakopi. Full L2/L3 orderbook reconstruction with imbalance analysis examples.

4. **lubluniky/bs-p** (118 stars, C) - AVX-512 ultra-low latency market making kernel. Followed by OctopusTakopi. Represents the cutting edge of HFT infrastructure.

5. **crypto-crawler/crypto-crawler-rs** (260 stars, Rust) - By soulmachine (followed by OctopusTakopi). The most comprehensive open-source multi-exchange data crawler.

### 7.4 Feature Gaps (Opportunities)

The following features from the target 26 have **no meaningful open-source implementations** found in this network:

- **Aggregated Open Interest** - No open-source aggregated OI across exchanges
- **Net Longs/Shorts Indicator** - No open-source implementation found
- **Volume Bubbles** (as trade-size visualization on charts) - No direct implementation
- **VWAP Suite** (multi-anchor VWAP) - Only basic VWAP in backtesting contexts

### 7.5 Cross-Network Connections

- OctopusTakopi follows **DegenSugarBoo**, who built OpenBook (the closest open-source analog to Bookmap)
- OctopusTakopi follows **hyperliquid-dex** and forked their order_book_server (L4 book data is unique to Hyperliquid)
- OctopusTakopi follows **soulmachine**, whose crypto-crawler-rs is the backbone for many data collection projects
- beinghorizontal follows **marketcalls**, whose openalgo platform supports 30+ brokers
- beinghorizontal forked **py-market-profile** by bfolkens and extended it into tpo_project
- OctopusTakopi forked and optimized **nkaz001/hftbacktest** collector for better performance
